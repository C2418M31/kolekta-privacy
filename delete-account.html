Delete your Kolekta account

Kolekta app by Synk Technologies

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ph.kolekta@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Kolekta account.
  2. In the email, include the mobile number you sign in with and your business name.
  3. We may reply to confirm that the request really comes from you.
  4. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days of confirming your request, and tell you when it is done.

Who can request it

Owners: deleting an owner account deletes the whole business, including its branches, staff accounts and receipts. Staff: ask your business owner to deactivate your account in the app, or email us with your business code and mobile number to request deletion of your own details.

What is deleted

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address, password and PIN.
  • Business details: business name, branches and staff accounts.
  • Receipt records and receipt photos, including the amounts, reference numbers, times and who logged them.

What may be kept

We may keep a limited record only where the law requires it, for example to resolve a legal dispute or meet a legal obligation. Crash reports collected by Firebase Crashlytics are not linked to your account details and are removed on the schedule set by Google.
