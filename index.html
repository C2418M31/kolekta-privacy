Kolekta Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Kolekta is a mobile app that helps small businesses in the Philippines record and review e-wallet and bank payment receipts. The app is provided by Synk Technologies ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Kolekta collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Who uses Kolekta

Kolekta has two kinds of accounts. Owners create a business account and manage branches and staff. Staff are added by an owner, sign in with a business code, mobile number and PIN, and log receipts for their branch.

2. Information we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Owner account details: name, business name, mobile number, password, and optionally an email address.To create your account, sign you in, and identify your business.
Staff details: name, mobile number, PIN, and the branch a staff member works at. These are entered by the owner.To let staff sign in and to show owners who recorded each payment.
Branch details: branch names and the business code.To organize payments by branch and let staff sign in to the right business.
Receipt information: the receipt photo, and the amount, reference number, bank or wallet, and time shown on the receipt, plus when it was logged and by whom. Receipts can contain the name or partial account number of the person who paid.To keep a payment log for the business, flag possible duplicates and receipts that need review, and let owners view and export their records.
Crash and device diagnostics: crash reports with the app version, device model, operating system version, and technical error details. These are collected by Firebase Crashlytics.To find and fix bugs and keep the app stable.

Kolekta does not connect to your GCash, Maya or bank accounts. It does not read your messages, contacts or location, and it does not process payments.

3. Camera and photos

Kolekta asks for camera permission so staff can photograph a payment receipt. The text on the photo is read on your device using Google ML Kit text recognition. The photo and the details you confirm are then sent to our servers and stored with that business's records.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and secure the app and your account.
  • To show owners the payments recorded by their staff and let them export their records.
  • To detect problems such as duplicate receipts.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Who we share information with

  • Within your business. An owner can see the receipts, photos and staff activity of their own business. Staff can see the receipts they have logged. Other businesses cannot see your data.
  • Service providers. We use cloud hosting providers to run our servers and store data, and Google (Firebase Crashlytics) to receive crash reports. They process data only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

6. Data storage and security

Data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted connections. We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, but no system is completely secure. Keep your password and PIN private, and ask your owner to reset a staff PIN if you think it is known by someone else.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active so you can access your payment records. When an account or business is deleted, we delete or anonymize its data, except where we must keep it to meet legal obligations.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or ask questions about how it is used. Owners can also deactivate staff accounts in the app. To make a request, including a request to delete your account and related data, email us at the address below and include the mobile number linked to the account. We may need to confirm your identity first. If you are in the Philippines, you also have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), including the right to complain to the National Privacy Commission.

9. Children

Kolekta is built for business use and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will notify users in the app or by other reasonable means.
